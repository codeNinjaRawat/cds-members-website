## Smart loader – approach
Patterns from modern checkout/banking UIs: delayed show (200 ms, avoids flash), minimum visible time + success frame, task-specific step list (people trust what they can follow), slow-network reassurance after 6 s, `role=status` polite announcements, progressbar semantics, reduced-motion support, overlay only for irreversible actions (payment), inline for small actions.
